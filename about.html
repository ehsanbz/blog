About Me

I'm Ehsan Bagherzadeh, an R&D engineer based in Berlin. I design electronic circuits and write firmware for microcontrollers, developing ideas through prototypes and careful iteration.

My background is in computer hardware engineering, with experience in industrial and medical technology. My main focus is the space where circuitry, microcontrollers, and code meet.

What I work on

  • Firmware development: Microcontroller programming, communication protocols, and hardware/software integration.
  • Circuit and PCB design: Electronic circuits, microcontroller systems, and device interfaces.
  • Supporting software: Desktop tools and clear interfaces for controlling devices and working with their data.

Research and development shapes my work and the way I approach everyday life: observe closely, ask questions, try an idea, and refine the details.

Beyond engineering

I enjoy photography, travel, and board games—especially games that bring people together around interesting decisions and good conversation. I'm also interested in Berlin's history and public transport, and in sharing stories that help people see the city from a different perspective.

About this website

This is a place for my work, interests, and ideas: engineering, technology, photography, board games, and life in Berlin. It also connects to my Persian blog and the other places where I share what I'm learning.
