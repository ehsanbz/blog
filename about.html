About Me

I'm Ehsan Bagherzadeh, a hardware and software developer based in Berlin. I design electronic systems and build the software that brings them to life, from microcontroller firmware to desktop applications.

My background is in computer hardware engineering, with experience in industrial and medical technology. I enjoy bringing electronics and code together to create practical tools, connected devices, and useful applications.

What I work on

  • Hardware development: Electronic circuits, PCB design, microcontrollers, and device interfaces.
  • Embedded software: Microcontroller firmware, communication protocols, and hardware/software integration.
  • Software development: Desktop applications and engineering tools for device control, data handling, and clear user interfaces.

My approach is practical and curious: understand the problem, design a clear solution, build it, and keep improving it.

Beyond engineering

I enjoy photography, travel, and board games—especially games that bring people together around interesting decisions and good conversation. I'm also interested in Berlin's history and public transport, and in sharing stories that help people see the city from a different perspective.

About this website

This is a place for my work, interests, and ideas: engineering, technology, photography, board games, and life in Berlin. It also connects to my Persian blog and the other places where I share what I'm learning.
